## Training Overview

### Objectives

By the end of training, users will be able to:
- ✅ Navigate the AR Control Hub confidently
- ✅ Perform daily tasks efficiently using the system
- ✅ Understand how to use key features for their role
- ✅ Know where to find help when needed
- ✅ Contribute to system adoption and success

### Training Approach

**Blended Learning:**
- Live instructor-led sessions (primary)
- Hands-on practice with demo data
- Video tutorials for self-paced learning
- Quick reference guides and job aids
- Ongoing Q&A support via Slack/Teams

**Learning by Doing:**
- 70% hands-on practice
- 20% instructor demonstration
- 10% theory and concepts

## Hands-On Exercises

### Exercise 1: Complete Collection Workflow (AR Specialist)

**Scenario:**
ABC Construction Supply is 75 days past due on invoice INV-002456 for $12,500. Your task is to work this account.

**Steps:**
1. Find ABC Construction in your worklist
2. Review customer 360 view
3. Check alert indicators
4. Review invoice detail
5. Add a phone call note documenting conversation
6. Customer promises $5,000 by Friday - log the promise
7. Send a payment reminder email
8. Create a task to follow up on Monday if no payment
9. Mark today's task as complete

**Time:** 15 minutes
**Success Criteria:** All steps completed with accurate data
